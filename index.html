We are high school and undergraduate students building serious research projects at the intersection of artificial intelligence and quantitative finance. We study how machine learning models, statistical methods, and large language models can be applied to real financial problems, from testing market efficiency to evaluating whether frontier AI can detect statistical arbitrage signals in live market data.

Omega7 Capital is not a club. We are a research collective. Every project published under our name has been reviewed, validated, and documented with the rigour the subject demands. Our members come from universities across Europe, Asia, Africa, and North America. What unites us is not our institution, but our standard.

Who we are

We are a diverse group of students from leading institutions worldwide, united by a passion for quantitative finance, machine learning, and applied AI. Our members have excelled in competitions such as ML Arena, where we ranked 1st with the lowest error score, and Sorbonne Université’s innovation competition, which we won with our AI-powered waste classification system, PlasticSense. Whether you're interested in quantitative finance, machine learning, or applied AI in the quant field, we offer a platform to collaborate, innovate, and publish high-quality research.

What we do

We design research programs around a central question, run them over four to six weeks, and publish the best work that comes out. Every individual project contributes to a collective final output: each piece of documented work feeds into a shared publication, reviewed and validated before it goes live. Our current focus is AI applied to quantitative finance: we test frontier language models on financial tasks, construct and evaluate statistical arbitrage strategies, and explore what machine learning genuinely adds when applied to real market data with rigorous out-of-sample controls. Our published research is available at omega7capital.org/research.
